But First, A Message from Our Pastor

Pastoral Outreach

“Assembly of God Pentecostal of Maculusso (ADPM Birmingham) proposes to develop an inclusive community support programme for residents of Newtown and the wider Birmingham area. The services will respond directly to priorities identified through conversations and consultations with local people.”Senior Pastor, ADPM Birmingham

For Children & Young People

01

Supervised Sports

Football, basketball and table tennis sessions that encourage physical activity, teamwork and positive friendships.

02

Music Tuition

Keyboard, guitar, drums and singing lessons to help young people develop creative skills, confidence and discipline.

03

Homework Club

Supplementary education supporting progress in English, mathematics, digital skills and other core subjects, particularly for children who experience educational disadvantage.

Older Residents

Befriending & Wellbeing

Regular befriending sessions, social gatherings, gentle activities and telephone welfare checks — reducing loneliness, strengthening community connections and giving residents the opportunity to form supportive friendships.

For All Ages

Intergenerational Activities

Cooking sessions and arts and crafts workshops give children, adults and older people the chance to learn creative skills, express themselves and improve their emotional wellbeing — with community celebrations and exhibitions to share their work with families and neighbours.

Employment & Welfare Support

Practical Support

ADPM Birmingham will also provide employment and welfare support, including help with job searches, CV writing, applications, interview preparation, digital access, benefits enquiries and referrals to specialist services. Volunteers will offer practical assistance and signpost residents to appropriate housing, health, debt and immigration support.

Activities will be open to people of all faiths and backgrounds. The church will work with schools, community organisations and statutory agencies to reach vulnerable residents, avoid duplication and ensure that local people continue to shape the services through feedback, surveys and regular consultation meetings.
